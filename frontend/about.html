About TEGO

Our story, vision, and commitment to Turkana

Our Story

The Turkana Eco-Green Organization (TEGO) was founded in 2020 by a group of passionate environmentalists and community development practitioners who recognized the urgent need for climate action in Turkana County.

What began as grassroots community mobilization around tree planting and environmental awareness grew into a fully registered Community-Based Organization (CBO) in 2023, with programs spanning environmental conservation, climate adaptation, food security, and water access.

Today, TEGO works with over 24 self-help groups and reaches more than 3,500 community members across Turkana County.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

A resilient and sustainable Turkana community that thrives in harmony with its environment, where every person has access to food, water, and opportunities for a dignified life.

Our Mission

To empower Turkana communities through climate resilience, sustainable natural resource management, and inclusive development programs that improve livelihoods and protect the environment.

Our Journey

2020

TEGO founded by community environmental activists in Turkana County.

2021

Launched first tree planting campaign, engaging 5 community groups and planting 10,000 seedlings.

2022

Expanded into food security programs, introducing poultry production and fodder farming.

2023

Officially registered as a Community-Based Organization. Launched Integrated Livestock-Crop Systems Project.

2024

Reached 3,500+ community members, established 24 self-help groups, and expanded water access programs.
