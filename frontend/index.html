About TEGO

The Turkana Eco-Green Organization (TEGO) is a community-based organization dedicated to building climate-resilient communities in Turkana County, Kenya. Founded in 2020 and officially registered in 2023, we work at the intersection of environmental conservation, food security, and sustainable livelihoods.

Our approach is rooted in community participation, recognizing that lasting change comes from empowering local people with the knowledge, tools, and resources they need to adapt to a changing climate.

Our Focus Areas

We address the most pressing challenges facing Turkana communities through integrated, community-led programs.

🌱

Environmental Conservation

Protecting and restoring ecosystems through tree planting, sustainable land management, and biodiversity conservation initiatives.

🌍

Climate Change Adaptation

Building community resilience through climate-smart practices, early warning systems, and disaster risk reduction strategies.

🌾

Food Security & Livelihoods

Promoting sustainable agriculture, livestock development, and income-generating activities for vulnerable households.

💧

Water, Health & Sanitation

Improving access to clean water, sanitation facilities, and hygiene education for healthier communities.
