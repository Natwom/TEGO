Integrated Livestock-Crop Systems Project

Our flagship initiative building resilient food systems in Turkana

Project Overview

The Integrated Livestock-Crop Systems Project addresses food insecurity and climate vulnerability by combining poultry production, fodder farming, and climate-smart agriculture into a unified, sustainable system.

By working with self-help groups, we create synergies between crop and livestock production that improve nutrition, generate income, and build resilience against climate shocks.

Project Components

🐔

Poultry Production

Establishing community poultry units with improved breeds, providing training on husbandry, disease management, and feed formulation for sustainable egg and meat production.

🌾

Fodder Farming

Cultivating drought-resistant fodder crops to ensure year-round livestock feed, reducing pastoralists' vulnerability to drought and improving animal health and productivity.

🌱

Climate-Smart Agriculture

Promoting conservation agriculture, agroforestry, and water-efficient irrigation techniques that increase yields while preserving soil health and water resources.

Key Activities

Self-Help Group Formation

Organizing community members into cohesive groups for collective action, resource pooling, and mutual support.

Training & Mentorship

Providing hands-on training in modern farming techniques, business skills, and leadership development.

Financial Literacy & Savings

Establishing village savings and loan associations (VSLAs) to build financial resilience and access to credit.

Market Linkages

Connecting producers with buyers, cooperatives, and markets to ensure fair prices and sustainable incomes.
