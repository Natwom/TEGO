Our Work

Programs transforming lives and landscapes in Turkana

What We Do

Our programs are designed to address the interconnected challenges of climate change, food insecurity, and environmental degradation.

Environmental Conservation

Protecting Turkana's fragile ecosystems through community-led conservation initiatives, reforestation, and sustainable land management practices.

  • Tree planting and nursery establishment
  • Anti-erosion measures and gully rehabilitation
  • Indigenous tree species preservation
  • Community forest management

Climate Change Adaptation

Building community resilience to climate shocks through early warning systems, climate-smart practices, and disaster preparedness.

  • Climate-smart agriculture training
  • Drought early warning systems
  • Alternative livelihood strategies
  • Community disaster risk committees

Food Security & Livelihoods

Improving household nutrition and income through sustainable farming, livestock development, and market linkages.

  • Poultry production systems
  • Fodder farming and pasture management
  • Vegetable gardening and irrigation
  • Market access and value chains

Water, Health & Sanitation

Ensuring access to clean water and promoting hygiene practices for healthier communities.

  • Borehole rehabilitation and maintenance
  • Rainwater harvesting systems
  • Hygiene and sanitation education
  • Community water management committees
